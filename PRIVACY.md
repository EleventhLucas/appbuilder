# Privacy

AppBuilder does not include telemetry, analytics, advertising, automatic diagnostic uploads, or update checking.

AppBuilder stores its project list, settings, and Codex conversation associations locally in its application-data directory. Project folders remain where you chose them.

When you use Codex, AppBuilder sends the conversation and any files you attach to the configured Codex service. That service's privacy terms apply to that use.

AppBuilder may keep a small local diagnostic log containing allowlisted operational metadata. It excludes prompts, conversation text, attachments, secrets, environment variables, command output, raw errors, URLs, executable paths, and project paths. Diagnostics leave the computer only when you explicitly export them.

Starting a project may open a local app in your default browser. AppBuilder does not publish projects or open them to the internet.
